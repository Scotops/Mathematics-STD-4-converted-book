Chapter One

Roman numbers
Introduction

In this chapter, you will learn to read and write numbers from 1 to 1,000 in Roman numbers. These numbers range from I to M in Roman numbers. They are used in various contexts such as on the introductory pages of books and on clock faces. The competencies developed will enable you to use Roman numbers in different contexts.
