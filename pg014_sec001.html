Roman numbers from XXXIV to L

Read the following Roman numbers: XXXIV, XXXV, XXXVI, XXXVII, XXXVIII, XXXIX, XL, XLI, XLII, XLIII, XLIV, XLV, XLVI, XLVII, XLVIII, XLIX and L.

Example 1

Write the following numbers in Roman numbers:

(a) 34 (b) 41 (c) 45

Solution

(a) 34= XXX + IV
= XXXIV
Therefore, 34 is XXXIV.

(b) 41 = XL + I
= XLI
Therefore, 41 is XLI.

(c) 45 = XL + V
= XLV
Therefore, 45 is XLV.
