Example 1

69 ÷ 3 =

Solution

23

Steps

1. Divide 6 by 3 to get 2.Write 2 above the tens place.

2. Multiply 2 by 3 to get 6.Write 6 below 6, then subtract: 6 − 6 = 0.

3. Drop 9 ones, then divide by 3 to get 3.Write 3 in the ones place to the right of 2.

4. Multiply 3 by 3 to get 9.Write 9 below 9, then subtract: 9 − 9 = 0.

Therefore, 69 ÷ 3 = 23.
