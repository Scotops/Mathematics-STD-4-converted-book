Solution
12 ⟌ 384
32
Steps
1.

Divide 3 by 12, it is not sufficient. Thus, take 38 then divide by 12. You will get 3 with remainder of 2. Write 3 in the tens place.

2.

Regroup 2 into 20 ones. Add ones: 20 + 4 = 24.

3.

Divide 24 by 12 to get 2. Write 2 in the ones place to the right of 3.

Therefore, the answer is 32.
