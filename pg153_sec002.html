Mathematics Pupil's Book Standard Four

Exercise 1

1. How many weeks and days are there in each of the following?

(a) 48 days
(b) 60 days
(c) 19 days
(d) 112 days

2. Convert the following time into days:

(a) 12 weeks 1 day
(b) 36 weeks 6 days
(c) 72 weeks 4 days
(d) 80 weeks

3. How many seconds are there in 2 hours and 30 minutes?

4. How many days are there in three leap years?

5. A bridge was constructed in 34 days.

Convert this time into weeks and days?

6. A bus travelled for 72 hours.

How many days did the bus travel?

7. The spinach in the vegetable garden took 30 days to mature.

Convert this time into weeks and days?

8. A teacher has two periods of 40 minutes for each lesson.

What is the total time of the periods in seconds?
