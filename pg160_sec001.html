Subtraction of measurements of time with regrouping

Measurements of time can be subtracted with regrouping. This should be done by considering the relationship existing between the units of time.

Example 1
hours
minutes
315
− 145

Steps

1. Subtract minutes: 15 − 45, it is not sufficient. Take 1 hour from the 3 hours, then convert it into 60 minutes. Add minutes: 60 + 15 = 75. Now, subtract minutes: 75 − 45 = 30. Write 30 in the minutes column. Remember that, two 2 hours remained in the hours column.

2. Subtract hours: 2 − 1 = 1. Write 1 in the hours column.
