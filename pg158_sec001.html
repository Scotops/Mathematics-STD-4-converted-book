Subtraction of measurements of time

Subtraction of measurements of time considers the relationship between the given units. For example, if it involves hours and minutes; subtract the minutes first, then hours.

Example 1
hours minutes
6 25
− 5 15
Steps

1. Subtract minutes: 25 − 15 = 10. Write 10 in the minutes column.

2. Subtract hours: 6 − 5 = 1. Write 1 in the hours column.

Solution
hours minutes
6 25
− 5 15
1 10
Therefore, the answer is 1 hour 10 minutes.
