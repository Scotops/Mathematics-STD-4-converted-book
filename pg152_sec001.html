Note that, division is applied when changing a small unit to a large unit, whereas multiplication is used when changing a large unit to a small unit.

Example 1

How many minutes are there in 720 seconds?

Solution

1 minute = 60 seconds

720 seconds=72060 minutes

=12 minutes

Therefore, there are 12 minutes in 720 seconds.

Example 2

A pupil takes 15 minutes to walk from home to school. Convert this time into hours.

Solution

1 hour = 60 seconds

15 minutes=1560 hours

Therefore, 15 minutes = 15/60 hours.

Example 3

How many minutes are there in 72 hours?

Solution

1 hour = 60 seconds

72 hours=72×60 minutes

=4320 minutes

Therefore, there are 4320 minutes in 72 hours.
