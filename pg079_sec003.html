Subtraction of measurements of volume

Metric units of volume with the same measurements can be subtracted. Start by subtracting smaller units followed by larger units.

Example 1
80 L 370 mL−45 L 160 mL=
Solution
L mL
80 370
− 45 160
210
Steps
1. Subtract millilitres:
370 mL−160 mL=210 mL.
Write 210 in the mL column.
