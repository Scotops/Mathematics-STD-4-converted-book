Addition of measurements of volume

Metric units of volume with the same measurements can be added. Start by adding smaller units followed by larger units.

Example 1
L mL
3 600
+ 4 450
Solution
L mL
3600
+ 4 450
50
L mL
+13600
+ 4 450
8 50
Steps
1. Add millilitres:
600 mL + 450 mL = 1050 mL. Convert 1050 mL to get 1 L and 50 mL.
Write 50 in the mL column and take 1 litre from the litre column.
2. Add litres:
1 L + 3 L + 4 L = 8 L.
Write 8 in the column of L.
Therefore, the answer is 8 L 50 mL.
