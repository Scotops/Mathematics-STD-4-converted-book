In subtracting decimal numbers, start with decimal parts followed by whole numbers.

Example 1

Steps
1.

Align the numbers vertically. Start by subtracting the numbers on the decimal side: 4−1=3. Write 3 in the decimal place.

2.

Subtract decimals: 8−2=6. Write 6 in the decimal place to the left of 3.

3.

Subtract whole numbers: 0−0=0. Write 0 in the whole number place.
