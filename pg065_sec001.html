Word problems involving measurements of length

Example 1

The length of a football ground is 100 metres. Convert this length into centimetres.

Solution

Convert 100 m into centimetre as follows:

1 m=100 cm
100 m=100 cm×100
=10000 cm

Therefore, the length of the football ground is 10,000 cm.

Example 2

Kija walked a distance of 8 kilometres and 700 metres from his home to the open market. Ashura walked a distance of 6,928 metres from school to her home.

(a) Who walked a longer distance?

(b) Find the difference in metres between the distances walked by Kija and Ashura.

Solution

(a) Kija walked 8 km and 700 m. Convert 8 km and 700 m into metres as follows.

1 km=1000 m
8 km=8×1000 m
=8000 m

Add 8000 m and 700 m.

8000 m+700 m=8700 m.
