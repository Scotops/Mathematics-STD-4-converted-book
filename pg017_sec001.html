Roman numbers

cannot be repeated more than three times when written to the right of a larger number. In numerals, these numbers correspond to 1, 10 and 100.

The numbers IV and IX represent ones. Therefore, when these numbers are written after higher value, then, the numbers are added to the larger number.

Example 1

Write the following Roman numbers in words:

(a) LXV
(b) LXXVI
(c) XCIX
(d) LIV
Solution
(a) LXV = 60 + 5
= 65
Therefore, the number LXV is sixty-five.
