Description

Use various programs such as Khan Academy to learn more about recognizing decimals with different place value and how to correctly perform addition and subtraction of decimals.

Summary

1. Decimals are read from left to right. The digits to the left of the decimal point are read as a whole number. The digits to the right of the decimal point are read one by one.

2. When adding or subtracting decimal numbers, consider the value of the digits or the place value of the digits before and after the decimal point.
