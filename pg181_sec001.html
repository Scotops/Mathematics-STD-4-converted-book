Example 2

A poultry farmer sold 38 eggs and earned a total of 17,126 shillings and 60 cents. What was the price of each egg?

Solution
Total sales = shs 17,126.60
Number of eggs = 38
Divide the sales by the number of eggs.
      shs   cts
38 ) 17126  60
     450    70

Therefore, the farmer sold each egg at 450 shillings and 70 cents.

Example 3

Eliakimu was paid a total of 68,763 shillings and 80 cents after working for one week. Calculate his daily wage.

Solution
Total payment = shs 68,763.80
Number of days = 7
Divide the payment by number of days.
Payments per day = shs 68,763.80 ÷ 7 = 9,823.40
= 9,823.40

Therefore, Eliakim’s daily wage is 9,823 shillings and 40 cents.
