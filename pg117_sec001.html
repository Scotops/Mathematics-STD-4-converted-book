Example 1

In a fraction chart, the shaded area represents the size of the fraction. Therefore, the shaded area in the chart can be used to compare fractions.
Example 1
Which fraction is greater between 12 and 14?
Solution
Examining the fraction chart, it can be seen that the shaded area representing 12 is greater than the shaded area representing 14.
Therefore, 12 is greater than 14.
Example 2
Which fraction is smaller between 26 and 25?
Solution
Examining the fraction chart, it can be seen that the shaded area representing 26 is smaller than the shaded area representing 25.
Therefore, 26 is smaller than 25.
