Exercise 6
1.

A parent gave a total of 333,500 shillings to 10 children. If this amount of money was shared equally, how much did each child get?

2.

A businessperson sold 452 oranges for a total of 146,900 shillings. If the oranges were sold at a uniform price, what was the selling price of each orange?

3.

Jamila bought 19 dresses for her business, spending a total of 463,144 shillings. If each dress had the same price, calculate the cost of a single dress.

4.

Amir sold 285 mangoes and earned a total of 119,913 shillings and 75 cents. If each mango was sold at the same price, what was the price of each mango?

5.

A group of 18 pupils shared 174,499 shillings and 20 cents equally. How much money did each pupil receive?

6.

A total of 52 passengers contributed 973,476 shillings and 40 cents for their tickets. If each passenger paid the same fare, what was the cost for one ticket?

7.

A teacher bought 87 mathematics books for 842,812 shillings and 50 cents. If each book had the same price, what was the cost of each book?

8.

A total of 224 seedlings were sold for 615,148 shillings and 80 cents. Calculate the price of a single seedling if each was sold at the same price.
