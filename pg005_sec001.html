Introduction

This textbook, Mathematics Pupil’s Book for Standard Four, is written in accordance with the 2023 Mathematics Syllabus for Primary Schools issued by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology. This is the second edition after the improvement of the Standard Four Textbook published in 2019, based on 2016 syllabus.

The book consists of ten chapters, namely, Roman numbers; Multiplication of whole numbers, Division of whole numbers; Measurements of length, mass and volume; Perimeters of plane figures; Area of plane figures; Fractions; Decimals; Measurements of time and Tanzanian Currency. The competencies developed will enable you to acquire basic mathematical skills and their applications, logical thinking and solve real life problems.

You are advised to engage yourself and with friends in all activities and exercises in this book in order to develop the intended competencies.
