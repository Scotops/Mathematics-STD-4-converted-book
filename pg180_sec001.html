Word problems involving the division of Tanzanian currency

Word problems involving the division of Tanzanian currency help to realize the application of division in real life.

Example 1

A pupil bought 17 exercise books for 14,790 shillings. How much did the pupil pay for each exercise book?

Solution

Cost of 17 exercise books = shs 14790

Number of exercise book=17

Divide the cost by the number of exercise books.

         870
17 ⟌ 14790
   -136
   ────
     119
    -119
    ────
       0

Therefore, the pupil bought each exercise book at 870 shillings.
