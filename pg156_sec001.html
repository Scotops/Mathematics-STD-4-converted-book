Example 1
hours minutes 3 45 + 4 35
hoursminutes 345 +435
Steps

1. Add minutes: 45 + 35 = 80. Convert 80 minutes into 1 hour and 20 minutes. Write 20 in the column of minutes. Take 1 hour to the column of hours.

2. Add hours: 1 + 3 + 4 = 8. Write 8 in the column of hours.

Solution
hours minutes +1 3 45 + 4 35 8 20
hoursminutes +1 345 +435
820

Therefore, the answer is 8 hours 20 minutes.

Example 2
hours minutes 4 32 + 2 28
hoursminutes 432 +228
Steps

1. Add minutes: 32 + 28 = 60. Convert 60 minutes into 1 hour and 00 minutes. Write 00 in the minutes column. Take 1 hour to the column of hours.

2. Add hours: 1 + 4 + 2 = 7. Write 7 in the hours column.

Solution
hours minutes +1 4 32 + 2 28 7 00
hoursminutes +1 432 +228
700

Therefore, the answer is 7 hours.
