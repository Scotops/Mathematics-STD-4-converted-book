Steps
1. Add the digits to the right of the decimal point: 7 + 6 = 13. Write 3 in the decimal place. The remaining whole number is 1. Shift 1 to the whole numbers position.
2. Add the whole numbers: 1 + 1 + 2 = 4. Write 4 in the whole number place.
1.7
+ 2.6
4.3
Therefore, 1.7 + 2.6 = 4.3.
