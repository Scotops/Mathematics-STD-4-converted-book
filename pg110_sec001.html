Description

Use online resources and programs such as GeoGebra and Khan Academy to learn more about the area of rectangles, squares, and triangles.

Summary

1.

The formula for finding the area of a triangle;

Area of a rectangle=length×width.

2.

The formula for finding the area of a square;

Area of a square=length×length.

3.

The formula for finding the area of a triangle;

Area of a triangle=12×base×height.
