Example 1
Write the following numerals in Roman numbers:
(a) 14 (b) 17 (c) 19
Solution
(a)\ 14 = X + IV
= XIV
Therefore, 14 is XIV.
(b)\ 17 = X + VII
= 17
Therefore, 17 is XVII.
(c)\ 19 = X + IX
= XIX
Therefore, 19 is XIX.
Example 2
Convert the following into numerals:
(a) XII (b) XV (c) XVIII
Solution
(a)\ XII = 10 + 2
= 12
Therefore, XII is 12.
(b)\ XV = 10 + 5
= 15
Therefore, XV is 15.
(c)\ XVIII = 10 + 8
= 18
Therefore, XVIII is 18.
