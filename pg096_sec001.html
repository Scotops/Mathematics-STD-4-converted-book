Activity 2
Recognising perimeters of plane figures using reliable ICT resources
Description
Use online programs and computer packages such as GeoGebra and Khan Academy to learn more on perimeters of plane figures and their applications.

Summary

1.

Perimeter is the length of the boundary of a plane figure.

2.

Perimeter of a rectangle = 2 × length + 2 × width.

3.

Perimeter of a square = 4 × length.

4.

Perimeter of a triangle = The sum of the lengths of all sides.
