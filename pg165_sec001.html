Example 3

A train leaves Dodoma at 0530 in the morning and arrives in Dar es Salaam at 0845 in the morning. How much time does the train take to travel from Dodoma to Dar es Salaam?

Solution

Departure time = 0530

Arrival time = 0845

Duration = Arrival time − Departure time

Subtract the measurements as follows:

hoursminutes
0845
− 0530
315
Eight hours forty-five minutes minus five hours thirty minutes equals three hours fifteen minutes.

Therefore, the train takes 3 hours 15 minutes to travel from Dodoma to Dar es Salaam.
