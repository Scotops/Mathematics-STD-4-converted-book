26.5 - 18.6 =
Steps

1. Subtract decimals: 5 – 6, it is not sufficient. Take 1 group of ones from the 6 ones and change into 10. Add decimals: 10 + 5 = 15. Subtract decimals: 15 – 6 = 9. Write 9 in the decimals place. Remember in tens place 1 tens remains.

2. Subtract whole number: 5 – 8, it is not sufficient. Add ones: 10 + 5 = 15. Subtract whole numbers: 15 – 8 = 7. Write 7 in the ones place. Remember in tens place, 1 tens remains.

3. Subtract whole numbers: 1 – 1 = 0.

Solution
\begin{array}{r}26.5\\-18.6\\\hline 7.9\end{array}
Therefore, 26.5–18.6 = 7.9.
