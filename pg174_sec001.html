Example 2

An exercise book is sold at 800 shillings.

What is the cost of 15 exercise books of the same type?

Solution

Cost of one exercise book = shs 800

Number of exercise books =15

Multiply the cost by the number of exercise books.

\text{shs }800 × 15 = 12000

Therefore, 15 exercise books will be sold at 12,000 shillings.
