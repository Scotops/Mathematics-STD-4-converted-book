Multiplication of three-digit numbers by a multiplier with up to two-digits

Example 1

217 × 4 =

Solution
Steps
217
× 4
868
1.

4 × 7 = 28. Write 8 in the ones place. Regroup 20 ones into 2 tens. Shift 2 tens to the tens place.

2.

4 × 10 = 40. Write 4 tens. Add tens: 4 + 2 = 6. Write 6 in the tens place.

3.

200 × 4 = 800. Write 8 in the hundreds place.

4.

Add the results from Step 1 to 3:
68 + 800 = 868

Therefore, 217 × 4 = 868.
