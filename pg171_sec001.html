Multiplication of currency in shillings and cents

Multiplication of currency in shillings and cents. The Tanzanian currency in shillings and cents can be multiplied by whole numbers. The cents are multiplied first followed by shillings. The relationship between shillings and cents must be used during the conversions. Note that, cents are represented by two digits and 1 shillings equals 100 cents. Example 1. 50 shillings 05 cents. multiplied by 6. Steps. Step number one. Multiply 5 cents by 6: 5 cents multiplied by 6 equals 30 cents. Write 30 in the cents column. Solution. 50 shillings 05 cents. multiplied by 6. 30. Step number two. Multiply 50 shillings by 6: 50 shillings multiplied by 6 equals 300 shillings. Write 300 in the shillings column. 50 shillings 05 cents. multiplied by 6. 300 30. Therefore, the answer is 300 shillings 30 cents or 300.30 shillings.
