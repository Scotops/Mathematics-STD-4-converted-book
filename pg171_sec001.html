Multiplication of currency in shillings and cents

The Tanzanian currency in shillings and cents can be multiplied by whole numbers. The cents are multiplied first followed by shillings. The relationship between shillings and cents must be used during the conversions.

Note that, cents are represented by two digits and shs 1 = 100 cents.

Example 1
shs cts
50 05
× 6
Steps
1.

Multiply 5 cents by 6: 5cts × 6 = 30cts Write 30 in the cents column.

Solution
shs cts
50 05
× 6
30
2.

Multiply 50 shillings by 6: shs 50 × 6 = shs 300. Write 300 in the shillings column.

shs cts
50 05
× 6
300 30
Therefore, the answer is shs 300 30 cts or shs 300.30.
