Conversion of small units of length into large units

Length measurements can be converted from small units to large units. Use multiplication (÷) method when converting small units into large units.

Example 1

Convert 500 cm into metres.
Solution

Use the relationship between metre and centimetre

1 m = 100 cm
500 cm=500 cm×1 m100 cm
=5 m
Therefore, 500 cm is equal to 5 m.

Example 2

Convert 4500 m into kilometres and metres.
Solution
Use the relationship between kilometre and metre
4500 m = 4000 m + 500 m
Convert 4000 m into kilometre:
1 km = 1000 m
4000 m=4000 m×1 km1000 m
=4 km
500 m is less than 1 km.
Therefore, 4500 m = 4 km 500 m.
