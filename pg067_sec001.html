Measurements of mass

The mass of an object is measured in metric units which include tonnes (t), kilograms (kg), hectograms (hg), dekagrams (dag), grams (g), decigrams (dg), centigrams (cg) and milligrams (mg).

Metric units of mass table. One gram equals ten decigrams, one hundred centigrams, or one thousand milligrams. One dekagram equals ten grams. One hectogram equals ten dekagrams or one hundred grams. One kilogram equals ten hectograms, one hundred dekagrams, one thousand grams, ten thousand decigrams, one hundred thousand centigrams, or one million milligrams.

1 tonne (t) = 1000 kilograms (kg)

From the table, the relationship between measurements can be established. For example, the following relationships can be easily established:

(a) 1 gram = 1000 milligrams
(b) 1 centigram = 10 milligrams
(c) 1 gram = 100 centigrams
(d) 1 kilogram = 1000 grams
