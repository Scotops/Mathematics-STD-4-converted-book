Addition of measurements of mass

Metric units of mass of the same measurement can be added. Start by adding smaller units followed by large units. The relationship between the given units should be considered.

Example 1

g mg
4 225
+ 4 370

Solution

g mg
4 225
+ 4 370
595
g mg
4 225
+ 4 370
8 595

Steps

1. Add milligrams:
225 mg + 370 mg = 595 mg.
Write 595 in the mg column.
2. Add grams:
4 g + 4 g = 8 g.
Write 8 in the g column.
Therefore, the answer is 8 g 595 mg.
