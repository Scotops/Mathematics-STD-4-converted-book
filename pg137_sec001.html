Conversion of fractions into decimals

Decimals are obtained by dividing the numerator by the denominator of a fraction.

Example 1

Convert 6/10 into decimals.

Steps

1.Divide the numerator by the denominator: 6 ÷ 10, it is not sufficient. Write 0 above the digit 6 in the answer position, then put a decimal point.

2.Multiply: 0 × 10 = 0. Write 0 below 6 then subtract: 6 - 0 = 6.

3.Write zero in front of 6 to make it 60, then divide: 60 ÷ 10 = 6. Write 6 in the answer position after the decimal point.

4.Multiply: 6 × 10 = 60. Write 60 below 60 then subtract: 60 - 60 = 0.

Solution
      0.6
10 ⟌ 6.0

      0

Therefore, 6/10 = 0.6.

Example 2

Convert 1/2 into decimals.

Steps

1.Divide the numerator by the denominator: 1 ÷ 2, it is not sufficient. Write 0 above the digit 1 in the answer position, then put a decimal point.

2.Multiply: 0 × 2 = 0. Write 0 below 1 then subtract: 1 - 0 = 1.

3.Write a zero in front of 1 to make it 10, then divide: 10 ÷ 2 = 5. Write 5 in the answer position after the decimal point.

Solution
      0.5

    10
