Word problems involving decimals

Decimal numbers are used in everyday activities in situation where more precision is required.

Example 1

A farmer divided the farm into three areas and planted various crops in each area. He planted 0.35 of the farm with sorghum, 0.25 with coffee and 0.20 with maize. Find the total area used to plant those crops.

Solution

Area planted with sorghum = 0.35.

Area planted with coffee = 0.25.

Area planted with maize = 0.20.

Total area planted with crops 0.35 + 0.25 + 0.20 = 0.80.

Therefore, the total area used to plant these crops is 0.80.

Example 2

The length of the rectangle is 102.75 centimetres and its width is 82.8 centimetres. Find the difference between the length and the width of the rectangle.

Solution

Length of the rectangle = 102.75 cm

Width of the rectangle = 82.8 cm

Difference between length and width:

102.75 cm – 82.8 cm = 19.95 cm

Therefore, the difference between the length and the width of the rectangle is 19.95 cm.
