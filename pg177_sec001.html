Division of currency in shillings and cents

The Tanzania currency in shillings and cents can be divided by whole numbers. The shillings are divided first followed by division of cents. Note that, the relationship between shillings and cents must be considered whenever conversion is needed.

Example 1
shs cts
5)10 50

Steps

1. Divide shillings 10 by 5: shs 10 ÷ 5 = shs 2. Write in the shillings place.
2. Divide cents 50 by 5: 50 ÷ 5 = cts 10. Write 10 in the cents place.

Solution
