Multiplication of fractions by whole numbers

This involves converting the whole number into fractions then followed by the rule of multiplying fractions by fraction.

Example 1
15×4=

Solution

When multiplying a whole number by a fraction, write the whole number as a fraction:

4=41

Then, multiply numerators and denominators separately:

15×4=15×41
=45
Therefore, 15×4=45.
