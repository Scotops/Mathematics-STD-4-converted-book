Example 1
Write the following Roman numbers in numerals:
(a) CMIX (b) DCCLXXIV
Solution

(a) CMIX = 900 + 9 = 909 Therefore, CMIX is 909.

(b) DCCLXXIV = 700 + 74 = 774 Therefore, DCCLXXIV is 774.
