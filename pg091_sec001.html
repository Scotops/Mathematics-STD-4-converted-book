The perimeter of a triangle. The perimeter of a triangle is the sum of the lengths of its three sides. Thus, the perimeter of a triangle is obtained by adding the lengths of its sides. Example 1. Find the perimeter of the following triangle. A triangle is shown. The left side is labelled 6 centimetres, the right side is labelled 7 centimetres, and the bottom side is labelled 9 centimetres. Solution. Perimeter of a triangle equals sum of the lengths of all sides. 6 centimetres 7 centimetres 9 centimetres. equals 22 centimetres. Therefore, the perimeter of the triangle is 22 centimetres. Example 2. A triangle has sides measuring 14 metres, 16 metres and 11 metres. Find its perimeter. Solution. Perimeter of a triangle equals sum of the lengths of all sides. equals 14 metres + 16 metres + 11 metres. equals 41 metres. Therefore, the perimeter of the triangle is 41 metres.

The perimeter of a triangle

The perimeter of a triangle is the sum of the lengths of its three sides. Thus, the perimeter of a triangle is obtained by adding the lengths of its sides.

Example 1

Find the perimeter of the following triangle.

A triangle is shown. The left side is labelled 6 centimetres, the right side is labelled 7 centimetres, and the bottom side is labelled 9 centimetres.

Solution

Perimeter of a triangle = sum of the lengths of all sides

6 cm7 cm9 cm

=22 cm

Therefore, the perimeter of the triangle is 22 cm.

Example 2

A triangle has sides measuring 14 metres, 16 metres and 11 metres. Find its perimeter.

Solution

Perimeter of a triangle = sum of the lengths of all sides

=14 m+16 m+11 m

=41 m

Therefore, the perimeter of the triangle is 41 m.
