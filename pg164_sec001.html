Word problems involving measurements of time

Word problems involving measurements of time show how they are used in real life.

Example 1

Nuru spends 1 hour and 20 minutes to wash dishes. She also spends 1 hour 35 minutes to water the garden. What is the total time spent by Nuru on these activities?

Solution

Time for washing dishes = 1 hour 20 minutes
Time for watering the garden = 1 hour 35 minutes
Add the time for the activities as follows:
hours minutes
1 20
+ 1 35
2 55

Therefore, Nuru spends 2 hours 55 minutes to do all activities.
