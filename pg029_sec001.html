Multiplication of two-digit numbers by a two-digit multiplier

Example 1
23×21=
Write multiplication problem vertically aligning the digits by place values
Solution
23× 2123+ 460483
Steps
1. 1×3=3. Write 3 in the ones.
2. 1×20=20. Write 2 in the tens place.
3. 20×3=60. Write 6 in the tens place and 0 in the ones place.
4. 20×20=400. Write 4 in the hundreds place.
5. Add the results from Steps 1 to 4: 23+460=483.
Therefore, 23×21=483.
