Mathematics Pupil's Book Standard Four

Exercise 1
1. Convert the following measurements:
(a) 17 km into metres
(b) 7 km 500 m into centimetres
(c) 35 cm into millimetres
(d) 7 dam into metres
2. A road is 22 kilometres long. Convert this length to decimetres.
3. Convert the following measurements:
(a) A farm of length 268 metres into millimetres
(b) A building with a height of 63 dam into metres
(c) A board with a length of 3 metres and 20 centimetres into millimetres
(d) A tower with a height of 15 hectometres into metres.
