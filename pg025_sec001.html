5. The Roman numbers V, L, and D cannot be subtracted in other numbers.

6. The Roman numbers I, X, and C can be subtracted from other numbers.

Therefore,

(a) I can be subtracted from V and X only, for example IV and IX.

(b) X can be subtracted from L and C only, for example XL and XC.

(c) C can be subtracted from D and M only, for example CD and CM.

7. The number I, X, and C can be written by repeating up to three times consecutively.

For example; III, XXX, and CCC.
