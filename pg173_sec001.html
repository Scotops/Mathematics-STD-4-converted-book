Word problems involving multiplication of Tanzanian currency

Word problems on multiplication of Tanzanian currency involve the application of the currency in real-life.

Example 1

A packet of tea leaves is sold at 500 shillings. Find the cost of buying 12 packets of tea leaves of the same type.

Solution

Cost of one packet = 500

Number of packets = 12

Multiply the cost of one packet by the number of packets
