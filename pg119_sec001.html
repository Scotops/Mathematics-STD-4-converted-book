Multiplication of fractions with different denominators

When multiplying fractions, the numerators and denominators are multiplied separately to obtain the answer.

Example 1
15×37=
Solution

Multiply the numerators by the numerator and the denominator by the denominator:

15×37=1×35×7=335
Therefore, 15×37=335.
