Chapter Seven

Fractions

Introduction

In this chapter, you will learn to compare, multiply and divide fractions with different denominators. You will also learn to multiply and divide fractions by whole numbers. The competencies developed will enable you to apply the concept of fractions in different contexts.

Comparing fractions

Fractions can be compared using various symbols. The equal sign (=), less than sign (<) and greater than sign (>) will be used to compare fractions in this aspect.

Equivalent fractions

Equivalent fractions are compared using the equal sign (=). Any fraction with the same value as the whole has a value of 1. For example,

22=33=44=55=66=1.
Note: When a fraction is multiplied by 1, its value does not change.
