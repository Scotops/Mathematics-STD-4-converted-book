The concept of area

The area is the surface region of the shape. The area can be determined by dividing the shape into smaller, equal squares and counting them. The area can also be obtained by using a formula and measurements. The area is measured in metric units such as square metres (m²), square centimetres (cm²) and square millimetres (mm²).

Area of a rectangle

The area of a rectangle is the entire surface region of the rectangle.
