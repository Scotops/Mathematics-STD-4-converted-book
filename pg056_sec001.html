Conversion of large metric units of length into small units

The measurements of length can be converted from large metric units to small units. The relationships of metric units of length are used in converting the units according to the needs. Use multiplication (×) method when converting larger units to smaller units.

Example 1
Convert 3 m into centimetres.
Solution
Use the relationship between metre and centimetre.
1 m = 100 cm
3 m = 100 × 3 cm
= 300 cm
Therefore, 3 m is equal to 300 cm.
Example 2
Convert 5 km 250 m into metres.
Solution
Use the relationship between kilometre and metre.
1 km = 1000 m
5 km = 1000 × 5 m
= 5000 m
Add 5000 m and 250 m.
Thus, 5000 m + 250 m = 5,250 m
Therefore, 5 km 250 m is equal to 5,250 m.
