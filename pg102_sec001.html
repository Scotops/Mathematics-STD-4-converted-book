Example 2

A book is 30 cm long and 20 cm wide. Find the surface area of the book.

Solution

The book has a rectangular surface.

length = 30 cm and width = 20 cm
Area of a rectangle = length × width
=30 cm×20 cm
=600 cm2

Therefore, the surface area of the book is 600 cm2.

Example 3

The width of the rectangle is 40 cm. If its length is 90 centimetres, find its area.

Solution
length = 90 cm
width 40 cm
Area of a rectangle = length × width
=90 cm×40 cm
=3600 cm2

Therefore, the area of a rectangle is 3600 cm2.
