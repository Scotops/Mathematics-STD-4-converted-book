Example 2

The height of a triangle is 15 metres. If its base is 30 metres, find the area of the triangle.

Solution

Height of a triangle = 15 m

Base of a triangle = 30 m

Area of a triangle = ½ × base × height

= ½ × 30 m × 15 m

= 225 m²

Therefore, the area of the triangle is 225 m².

Example 3

Find the area of the triangle KLM.

Solution

Base (KM) = 26 cm

Height (LN) = 12 cm

Area of the triangle KLM = ½ × height × base

= ½ × 12 cm × 26 cm

= 156 cm²

Therefore, the area of the triangle KLM is 156 cm².
