Multiplication of two-digit numbers by a one-digit multiplier

Example 1

62×4=

Steps
1.

Multiply 2 ones by 4: 2 × 4 = 8. Write 8 in the ones place.

2.

Multiply 6 tens by 4: 6 × 4 = 24. Write 4 in the tens place. Regroup 20 tens into 2 hundreds.

Therefore, 62 × 4 = 248.

Example 2

25×6=

Steps
1.

Multiply 5 ones by 6: 5 × 6 = 30. Write 0 in the ones place. Shift 3 tens to the tens place.

2.

Multiply 2 tens by 6: 2 × 6 = 12. Add tens: 2 + 3 = 5. Write 5 in the tens place. Regroup 10 tens into 1 hundreds. Write 1 in the hundreds place.

Therefore, 25 × 6 = 150.
