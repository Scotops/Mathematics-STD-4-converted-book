Word problems involving measurements of volume

Word problems involving measurements of volume highlight the use of metric units of volume in real-life situations. They reflect the activities performed every day.

Example

A cow produces 20 litres of milk every day. How many millilitres of milk will the cow produce in 3 days?

Solution

1 Day: 20 litres

3 days=3×20 L=60 L

Convert 60 L into millilitres as follows.

1 L=1000 mL

60 L=60×1000 mL

=60000 mL

So, the cow will produce 60,000 millilitres of milk in 3 days.
