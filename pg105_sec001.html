Example 2

A square side field has a length of 70 metres. Find the area of the field.

Solution

Side length = 70 m

The square is drawn as follows.

70 m ↔
↕ 70 m

Thus, area of a square = length × length.

= 70 m × 70 m

= 4900 m².

Therefore, the area of the field is 4900 m².
