Word problems involving multiplication of whole numbers

Example 1

A businessman bought 170 boxes of bar soap. Each box contains 20 bars of soap. How many bars of soap were bought in total?

Solution

1 box contains 20 bars of soap.

170 boxes contain 170 × 20 = 3400 bars.

Therefore, the businessman bought a total of 3,400 bars of soap.

Example 2

The school bought 5624 crates of soda. If each crate had 24 bottles of soda, how many bottles of soda were bought in total?

Solution

Number of crates 5,624

Each crate contained 24 bottles of soda.

5,624 × 24 = 22,496 + 112,480 = 134,976

Therefore, the school bought a total of 134,976 bottles of soda.
