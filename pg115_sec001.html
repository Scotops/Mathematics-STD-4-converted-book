Example 3

Use the equal sign (=) to recognise equivalent fractions from the following list:

818,25,49,1845

Solution

Relate fractions by multiplying the fraction with a smaller denominator by a fraction with the same numerator and denominator as follows:

25=25×99=1845

49=49×22=818

Therefore, 25=1845 and 49=818.
