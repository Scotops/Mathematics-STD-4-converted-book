Area of a square

The area of a square is the entire surface region of the square. The area of a square is obtained by multiplying the length by the width of the square. Recall that all sides of a square have equal length.

Thus, Area of a square = length × width or length × length.

Example 1
Find the area of the following square.
The diagram shows a square. Each side is 9 centimetres long.
Solution
Length of a side = 9 cm
Area of the square = 9 cm × 9 cm
= 81 cm².
Therefore, the area of the square is 81 cm².
